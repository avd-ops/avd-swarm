# avd-assistant's icon: the owner's decision

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** Replace the Clippy-style paperclip mascot (`assets/buddy/avd-buddy-icon-source.jpg`) with the vector **Shield + `><` visor** brand kit (`assets/assistant/svg/icon-mark-transparent.svg` and `assets/assistant/BRAND.md`), using `avd-ops` Design System 3.0 tokens (`#1ca998`, `#0d1015`).
- **Why:** Moving from `avd-buddy` to `avd-assistant` (where Buddy is one of four interactive modes) drops the Clippy resemblance and trademark baggage recorded in [`assets/buddy/REVIEW.md`](../buddy/REVIEW.md), and scales cleanly at 32px favicon and 512px maskable PWA sizes.
- **Public demo:** `assistant.avd-ops.dev` (`?sim=1` synthetic fixtures only, research-only notice first, no external fonts or scripts).
